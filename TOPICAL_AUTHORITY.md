# Topical Authority — ruangan.kacafilm.co.id

## Role and boundary

Repository evidence identifies ruangan.kacafilm.co.id as a legacy redirect shell to `kacafilm.co.id`, not as an independently evidenced product or installation website. Its only specific legacy route maps `kaca-film-ruangan-<kota>` to `kaca-film-gedung-<kota>`, so the defensible editorial scope is room-focused education within the broader building-window-film category. The hub helps owners, designers, facility managers, contractors, and procurement teams match room use cases to film requirements, evidence, installation, and care. It must not assert product construction, VLT/UV/IR/TSER, privacy, daylight, glare, heat, compatibility, indoor-air performance, standards, warranty, or pricing without current evidence.

## Context used

- No sitemap XML exists. Context therefore comes from the compact homepage, redirect map, README, and route/config evidence permitted by lean mode.
- The repository contains one `index.html`, a 26-byte README naming the hostname, `_redirects`, two Go maintenance scripts, and two manual GitHub Actions workflows.
- `index.html` is empty HTML5 boilerplate and supplies no visible product, room, or service copy.
- `_redirects` sends the root to `https://kacafilm.co.id` with a 301 and maps `/2021/01/kaca-film-ruangan-:kota.html` to `https://kacafilm.co.id/kaca-film-gedung-:kota`.
- The redirect pattern supports room/building window-film education plus consolidation of legacy location pages; it does not substantiate specific products, metrics, certifications, installers, or commercial terms.
- Mode lean is retained. Construction/specification, room suitability, comfort/privacy, compatibility, installation, indoor-air, maintenance, certification, warranty, and pricing are publication gates.

## Ignored template noise

- The dynamic city token in the legacy redirect is treated as location-template noise, not as a set of city topics.
- The boilerplate title “HTML 5 Boilerplate” and empty body are not semantic evidence.
- Generic Adsense/sitemap scripts and workflow files are implementation utilities, not product or quality evidence.
- The tiny README adds no context beyond the hostname and is not extrapolated into service or performance claims.

## Internal-link rule

Setiap artikel menaut ke hub topiknya, satu artikel pendamping dalam topik yang sama, dan satu atau dua topik tetangga yang mendukung keputusan berikutnya. Artikel room-use dapat mengarah ke situs komersial kanonis setelah rute final dikonfirmasi; tidak ada artikel baru berbasis kota. Pembahasan film construction, VLT/UV/IR/TSER, daylight/privacy/glare/heat, glass compatibility, thermal stress, measurement, cure, indoor-air/odor/VOC, maintenance interval, standard, certification, warranty, dan price harus menahan angka atau klaim sampai bukti produk/proyek aktual tersedia.
